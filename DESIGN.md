# Hairroom

A quiet, editorial hair consultation studio. A useful workspace with the warmth of an independent salon's printed lookbook.

## Color
- Canvas #f7f6f2, surfaces #ffffff, ink #30362d.
- Olive #6b7558 is the single primary action color; pale moss #eef0e7 for selected states.
- Warm gray #85877d for secondary text, fine borders #e7e7df.

## Typography
- Latin wordmark in Georgia; Chinese headlines in Noto Serif SC / Songti SC / SimSun.
- Interface in PingFang SC / Microsoft YaHei / system sans-serif, with generous line height.
- Small uppercase Latin captions are used sparingly to structure the studio.

## Layout
- 208px sidebar, max-width 1360px workspace; on mobile the sidebar becomes a compact top bar.
- Desktop working area: a 270px photo/preferences column and three portrait recommendations.
- Mobile: photo first, then stacked recommendations; controls wrap and never horizontally clip.

## Components
- Square portrait panels and restrained 10–16px corners. Subtle lines, minimal shadows.
- Native inputs and labelled selectors; keyboard-visible focus; dialog focus containment.
- Demo photography always labelled AI-generated sample. Real hairstyle photos on the homepage come from Unsplash and show the photographer credit, linked to the original. User images have explicit transfer consent before real try-on.
- Do not fabricate face analysis, scores, progress, or generated user previews.
